# Free Former — Four Brand Styles: Review & Enhancement Brief

## Your task

You are reviewing and improving four candidate brand styles for **Free Former**. Each style has a brand board in `reference/`, and the fourth also has four example posts. They are working drafts built as static HTML and open directly in a browser.

1. Read this whole brief, then open every file in `reference/`.
2. Review each style with the design skills available to you, for example:
   - a design critique (hierarchy, consistency, usability)
   - an accessibility review (contrast, text size)
   - typography and colour-palette checks
   - an anti-slop or visual-noise pass
   - spacing and layout rhythm

   For each style, write findings first in `review/[style].md`, ranked by impact.
3. Enhance each style in `enhanced/`:
   - one brand board per style at 1920×1080
   - at least three Instagram posts per style at 1080×1350

   Keep each style recognisably itself. Push quality, not direction.
4. Finish with `enhanced/SUMMARY.md`. It should give, per style, the three biggest changes and why, and list any open questions for the owner.

Work style by style. Show me the review for one style before enhancing it if anything in it would change the direction.

## Hard constraints (all four styles)

- **The logo is fixed.** Use only the files in `assets/`:
  - an ornate Art Nouveau monogram (interlocked F's)
  - a matching display wordmark

  Each comes in Forest (`*-forest.png`) and cream (`*-ground.png`). These were cut from a screenshot and are low-res, so leave a note where the vector originals should replace them. Never redraw, recolour beyond these two inks, distort or recreate the logo in a font.
- **Tagline:** "Practice without permission." Always set in DM Sans Italic, tracked about +6–8%, matching the live site (freeformer.ca).
- **Core palette from the live site:**

  | Name | Hex | Role |
  |---|---|---|
  | Forest | `#3B443A` | Logo ink |
  | Linen | `#DDD6CA` | Site background |
  | Ground | `#EEE9DF` | Light background |
  | Deep | `#1E201C` | Dark background |
  | Moss | `#5C6B58` | Secondary |
  | Stone | `#6F6D67` | Body text on the site |

  Each style adds at most one or two accents, listed under that style.
- **Body type:** DM Sans. DM Mono is used for labels and stamps.
- **Unknown facts stay as `[PLACEHOLDERS]`.** This covers price, dates, number of founding spots, access hours, promo code, handle and testimonials. Never invent numbers, offers or quotes.
- **Business model:** the studio is unstaffed and self-led. There are no group classes and no instructor in shots. Copy and imagery must work for one person alone.
- **Privacy is a selling point.** Avoid full, identifiable faces in pre-launch content; prefer crops at the joints, blur or backs. The exception is Style 4's focus-window post, where the face is deliberately blurred.
- **Photography:** the grey boxes and drawn figures in `reference/` are stand-ins. Keep them as clearly labelled placeholders (what to shoot) or improve the stand-ins. Don't use stock photos of identifiable people.
- **Never add:** emoji, gradient washes, glossy gym imagery, neon, or "burn"/intensity language.
- **Accessibility:** text must meet WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1, or 3:1 at 24px and above). Captions must not drop below 12px at export size.

## Brand context

- **What it is:** Free Former is an unstaffed reformer Pilates studio on Queen Street West, Toronto.
- **Opening:** grand opening planned for November; it's currently pre-launch.
- **The model:** members let themselves in, pick a session from a library of guidance material, and practise privately.
- **The live site:** freeformer.ca is a cream fabric-texture page with the Forest monogram and wordmark, the tagline, and one paragraph. Its message: a self-led model with a bank of guidance material, offering a quiet, private space to build a routine.
- **Near-term content goals:** warm up the Instagram account, build a following, then promote the Founders membership presale and the launch.
- **Competitive note:** KŌR Modern Pilates is a local GTA competitor that owns "high-intensity, dark and sleek". Free Former should differentiate on privacy and self-direction, not intensity.

## Style 1 — After Hours (from KŌR Modern Pilates + Salud Studios)

**Concept:** the studio after dark, presented like a fashion label rather than a gym. Sleek, cinematic and confident, but quiet.

**Borrowed traits:**
- **From Salud:**
  - near-black cinematic interiors lit by LED strips and window light
  - the logomark living inside the photography, cast as a shadow or etched on equipment
  - wide-tracked condensed caps
  - one centred label for the founding-memberships launch, plus a date line
  - monospace detail lines on space reveals
  - deadpan lowercase one-line captions
  - offline objects left in the neighbourhood and photographed in place
- **From KŌR:**
  - a named signature method with its own highlight
  - lifestyle formats: notes-app checklist, playlist cover, receipt layout
  - one fixed caption sign-off
  - every movement post paired with a quieter texture post

**Palette:**

| Name | Hex | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Deep | `#1E201C` | ~45% |
| Forest | `#3B443A` | |
| Ground | `#EEE9DF` | type |
| Linen | `#DDD6CA` | |
| Moss | `#5C6B58` | only in real objects (plants, tile) |

**Type:**
- Barlow Condensed SemiBold, tracked caps, for statements
- DM Mono for detail lines ("WALNUT · SPRINGS · DAYLIGHT")
- DM Sans for body

**Key applications:**
- a statement cover with the monogram as a wall shadow
- a "[NUMBER] FOUNDING MEMBERSHIPS." launch slide
- a "REFORMER." space reveal

**Voice:** lowercase, deadpan, one line ("let yourself in.").

## Style 3 — The Light Hours (from Luces Living)

**Concept:** one ownable visual metaphor, light. A flat sun disc marks the hour a member chooses to practise: a low sun for early sessions, a high sun at midday, a small moon for late ones. The brand sells belonging and meaning, not workouts.

**Borrowed traits (from Luces Living):**
- a single metaphor carried through every post
- editorial title slides in wide-tracked caps with a dated footer
- information posts designed like magazine pages
- mixed-media carousels (video and stills)
- colour-block tiles as punctuation for launches

## Known weak spots to look at first

- **Type sizes:** several labels in the boards are 9–11px at board size. Check them at real export size, and bump or cut them.
- **Drawn stand-ins:** the drawn figures in Style 4 are rough; either refine them as clean silhouettes or turn them into labelled photo placeholders.
- **Contrast:**
  - the Brass and Low Sun swatch labels
  - text over the halftone
  - the outlined titles on light grey in the Align post
- **Spacing:** rhythm differs between boards. Unify margins, gutters and section-label treatment across all four so they can be compared side by side.
- **Separation between styles:** Styles 1 and 4 are both dark. Make sure they read as clearly different (fashion-label sleek vs. grainy editorial collage).
