# Mozilla Common Voice

- **Source:** Mozilla
- **Link:** https://commonvoice.mozilla.org
- **License:** CC0
- **Size:** Luganda: ~560 h recorded, ~437 h validated, 672 speakers (Common Voice 26.0, per team research)
- **Layer:** common (brief 7.3 A)
- **Used for:** build: Luganda speech for testing speech recognition
- **Synthetic:** no
- **Status:** reviewed

## What it is

Crowdsourced voice recordings covering many African languages (brief 7.3). The brief describes it as CC0; verify on the dataset page.

## How we use it

Candidate source of Luganda clips for WER testing (RQ3.1) and code-switching examples (RQ3.2).

## What this does not cover

- Read-aloud sentences, not clinical conversations.
