## What it is

Maternal health question and answer pairs in four languages, including Luganda. Text only.

## How we use it

Seeds the Luganda symptom glossary (with English loanwords) and gives a text test set for the labeler (PR5, D37).

## What this does not cover

- Text only: no speech, accents or noise.
- Maternal health only; no child illness, chronic disease or general adult complaints.
