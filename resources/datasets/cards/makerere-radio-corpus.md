# Makerere Radio Speech Corpus

- **Source:** Makerere University (Mukiibi et al., 2022)
- **Link:** Zenodo (access restricted; request access)
- **License:** CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 per the paper (Zenodo metadata says CC BY 4.0; we follow the stricter one)
- **Size:** 155 h, of which 20 h human-transcribed
- **Layer:** ours (named in team research)
- **Used for:** build (test only)
- **Synthetic:** no
- **Status:** reviewed

## What it is

Luganda radio broadcast speech, natural rather than read, with some Luganda–English code-switching.

## How we use it

Test only, for natural speech and code-switching (RQ3.1, RQ3.2). No derivatives, so we never train or fine-tune on it.

## What this does not cover

- Broadcast speech, not patients describing symptoms; little clinical vocabulary.
- Radio presenters and callers, not older, rural or unwell speakers in a noisy waiting room.
- Luganda only; no Lusoga.
